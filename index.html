Friday, November 20 – Sunday, November 22, 2026 · Prepared for troop leadership review · Group: about 7 Scouts and 4–5 adult leaders · Vehicles: 3, one towing the troop trailer · Base camp: Greenbelt Park Campground (National Park Service, Greenbelt, MD) — campsites are reserved

Must-sees: the Great Hall (one of the most beautiful public rooms in America) · the Main Reading Room from the visitors' gallery · Thomas Jefferson's own library — the books that restarted the collection · a Gutenberg Bible, one of only three perfect vellum copies in existence.

6. How we pay for the Metro — the three-tier plan

Saturday is exactly two paid Metro rides per person — in via Capitol South in the morning, out via Foggy Bottom at night — at weekend fares of $2.50 each way, $5.00 per person. WMATA's Tap. Ride. Go. lets riders tap a contactless bank card or phone wallet at the faregate: no Smart Trip card, no app, no signup.

  • Tier 1 — Adults and Scouts with phones (the default): before the trip, a parent adds their contactless debit/credit card to the Scout's own Apple Pay / Google Pay / Samsung Pay. She taps the same card on the same phone in and out; the fare charges per ride to the parent's card and shows as "Metro." iPhone: set that card as the Express Transit card. Do not also put a Smart Trip card on the same phone — the gate reads Smart Trip first.
  • Tier 2 — Scout with no phone, parent on the trip: the parent taps her in and out on a different card in the parent's own wallet, selected by hand at each tap. One card can never carry two riders, so one spare card covers one Scout, and the parent keeps a note of which card belongs to which Scout.
  • Tier 3 — Scout with no phone and no parent on the trip: a leader buys a physical Smart Trip card at the Greenbelt station fare machines ($10 = $2 card fee + $8 loaded; ~$3 remains after Saturday, and the card becomes a troop spare). We expect only 1–2 Scouts here.

Gate rules for everyone: one rider per card; present one card at a time (tapping a whole wallet causes "card clash" — the wrong card, or both, can be charged); and always tap out with the exact same card and device used to tap in.

What we still need from families: the list of Scouts who will not have a phone on the trip, and which of them won't have a parent along — that sorts Tier 2 from Tier 3 and tells the leader how many physical cards to buy.
