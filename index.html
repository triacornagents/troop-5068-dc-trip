Arlington National Cemetery — Sunday

When: Sunday — leave camp 8:00, arrive ~8:45, guard change 10:00 AM (11:00 fallback), depart ~11:30

Reservations: none — entry is free; security screening at the Welcome Center, 1 Memorial Ave. Adults: photo ID.

Getting there: we DRIVE (it's on the way home) — full convoy with the trailer.

The plan:

  • Welcome Center → Tomb is ~1 mile, mostly uphill — we're settled on the plaza by 9:45
  • Changing of the Guard — hourly in winter. Sentinels walk 21 steps, pause 21 seconds (the 21-gun salute); the Tomb has been guarded around the clock since 1937
  • Kennedy gravesite & Eternal Flame — 7–10 min downhill from the Tomb

Scout briefing: an active cemetery — total silence on the Tomb plaza, stand when asked, phones away (Sentinels correct loud crowds), yield to funeral processions, stay on paved paths.

Open items: trailer parking — call ANC 877-907-8585 to confirm before the trip (buses/RVs use the top deck, ~$10/hr, $40 max; trailers aren't in the published guidance). Wreath ceremony — Bethany + Harold's call; the troop would supply the wreath.

Metro & Money

Saturday = 2 rides per person: Greenbelt → Capitol South (morning) · Foggy Bottom → Greenbelt (night)

Cost: $2.50 each way = $5.00 per person (weekend fares)

How: Tap. Ride. Go. — tap a contactless card or phone wallet at the gate. No Smart Trip card, no app, no signup.

Parking: Greenbelt station lot is free on Saturdays. The trailer stays at camp.

The three tiers:

  • Tier 1 — Scout has a phone (most): a parent adds their debit/credit card to the Scout's own phone wallet before the trip. She taps the same card on the same phone in and out. iPhone: make it the Express Transit card. Don't also add a Smart Trip card to that phone.
  • Tier 2 — no phone, parent on the trip: parent taps her in/out on a different card in the parent's wallet (never the parent's own ride card — one card, one rider). Parent keeps a note of which card is whose.
  • Tier 3 — no phone, no parent: a leader buys a physical Smart Trip card at Greenbelt station ($10 = $2 fee + $8 loaded; ~$3 left after Saturday; becomes a troop spare). Expect 1–2 Scouts.

Gate rules: one card per tap — present one card alone (tapping a whole wallet causes "card clash"); tap out with the exact card and device you tapped in with; don't remove/re-add cards or switch wallet apps mid-day.
